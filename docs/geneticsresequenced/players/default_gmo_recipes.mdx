# Default GMO Recipes

[GMO Cells](@genetics_resequenced:gmo_cell) can be made with a specific Gene in mind, rather than faffing about with weights and chances and such.

They are made in a *low temperature* [Advanced Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:advanced_incubator). Success chance is lowered with [Overclockers](@genetics_resequenced:overclocker), but raised with Chorus Fruit.

The recipes can be seen in-game with [EMI](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/emi), but many modpacks don't have it. While I work on JEI support, you can see the list of default recipes here:

## Cell Growth recipes

These recipes take a [Potion of Cell Growth](@genetics_resequenced:cell_growth) set to a particular entity type, plus another ingredient.

These recipes don't let you get any unique Genes, they just give you a way to get common Genes without relying on randomness.

| Gene | Entity Type | Ingredient | Chance of Success |
|------|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| Bioluminescence | Magma Cube | Glowstone Dust | 85% |
| Bioluminescence | Blaze | Blaze Powder | 85% |
| Emerald Heart | Villager | Emerald | 85% |
| Keep Inventory | Shulker | Emerald Block | 45% |
| Lay Eggs | Chicken | Egg | 100% |
| Luck | Rabbit | Emerald | 75% |
| Meaty | Pig | Raw Porkchop | 100% |
| More Hearts | Enderman | Golden Apple | 20% |
| Photosynthesis | Mooshroom | Mushroom Stem | 70% |
| Regeneration | Iron Golem | Golden Apple | 30% |
| Speed | Rabbit | Golden Boots | 65% |
| Teleport | Enderman | Ender Pearl | 45% |

## Mutation recipes

These recipes take a [Potion of Mutation](@genetics_resequenced:mutation) set to a particular entity type, plus another ingredient.

These are the only way to get some of the more unique Genes.

|------|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| Claws II | Polar Bear | Diamond Sword | 75% |
| Efficiency IV | Silverfish | Netherite Pickaxe | 25% |
| Scare Spiders | Spider | Fermented Spider Eye | 50% |
| Speed II | Rabbit | Diamond Boots | 50% |
| Resistance II | Shulker | Netherite Chestplate | 50% |
| Haste II | Rabbit | Netherite Pickaxe | 35% |
| Flight | Ender Dragon | Elytra | 55% |
| More Hearts II | Enderman | Golden Apple | 25% |

| Meaty II | Pig | Blaze Powder | 75% |

Do note that after getting a successful GMO Cell, you can duplicate it with a [Potion of Cell Growth](@genetics_resequenced:potion_of_cell_growth)!
