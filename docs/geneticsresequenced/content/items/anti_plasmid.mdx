<PrefabObtaining/>

The **Anti-Plasmid** is used to remove unwanted Genes from a host.

Craft an empty Anti-Plasmid with any completed regular [Plasmid](@genetics_resequenced:plasmid) to get an Anti-Plasmid of the same Gene.

Put it through the [Plasmid Injector](@genetics_resequenced:plasmid_injector) the same way as a regular Plasmid, and then when it's injected into the host, it will remove the Gene from them if they have it.
