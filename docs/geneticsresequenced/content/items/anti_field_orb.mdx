The **Anti-Field Orb** can be toggled to disable certain Genes while held in your inventory.

Simply right-click the Orb to toggle it on or off.

It can disable the following Genes:
- [Item Magnet](@genetics_resequenced:item_magnet)
- [XP Magnet](@genetics_resequenced:xp_magnet)
