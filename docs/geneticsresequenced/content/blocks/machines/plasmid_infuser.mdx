The **Plasmid Infuser** is used to fill [Plasmids](@genetics_resequenced:plasmid) with Genes from [DNA Helices](@genetics_resequenced:dna_helix).

Insert a Plasmid in the right slot, and DNA Helices in the left slot.

Each Gene has a specific amount of DNA Points required for the Plasmid to be infused with that Gene.

The first DNA Helix infused into the Plasmid will determine the Gene that the Plasmid will have. Each Helix after that will contribute DNA Points.

Helices with the Basic Gene will contribute 1 point, and Helices matching the Plasmid's Gene will contribute 2 points.

## Example

The [Eat Grass](@genetics_resequenced:eat_grass) Gene requires 16 DNA Points.

First, put an empty Plasmid into the right slot.

Then, insert an Eat Grass DNA Helix into the left slot. This will set the Plasmid's Gene to Eat Grass, but not contribute any points.

Each Eat Grass Helix inserted after that will contribute 2 points. Basic Helices will contribute 1 point.

After you've inserted 16 points worth of Helices, the Plasmid will be completed and can be removed and used in the [Plasmid Injector](@genetics_resequenced:plasmid_injector).
