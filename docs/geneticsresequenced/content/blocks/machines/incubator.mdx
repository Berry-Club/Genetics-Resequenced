The **Incubator** is used for certain recipe types.

It works similarly to a Brewing Stand, and can also do Brewing recipes!

There are three slots on bottom and one on top. The ingredient on top is shared by all 3 slots on the bottom, so it can craft up to 3 items at a time.

For example, you can put up to 3 Mundane Potions in the bottom slots, and any Organic Matter in the top slot, and it will produce 3 Organic Substrate potions.
