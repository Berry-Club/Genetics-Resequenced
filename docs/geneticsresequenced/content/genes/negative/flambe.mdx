The **Flambe** Gene will constantly light the entity on fire.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Fire Proof](@genetics_resequenced:fire_proof) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
