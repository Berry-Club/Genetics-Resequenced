The **Slowness** Gene gives entities the Slowness potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Speed](@genetics_resequenced:speed) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).

Slowness IV requires Speed II, and Slowness VI requires Speed IV.
