The **Blindness** Gene gives entities the Blindness potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Night Vision](@genetics_resequenced:night_vision) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
