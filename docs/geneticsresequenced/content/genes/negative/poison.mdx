The **Poison** Gene gives entities the Poison potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Poison Immunity](@genetics_resequenced:poison_immunity) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).

The Poison IV Helix can be obtained by combining a Potion of Viral Agents with a [Wither Hit](@genetics_resequenced:wither_hit) DNA Helix in the Incubator.
