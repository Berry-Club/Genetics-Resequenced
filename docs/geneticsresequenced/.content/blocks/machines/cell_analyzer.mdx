<PrefabObtaining/>

The **Cell Analyzer** is used to convert [Organic Matter](@genetics_resequenced:organic_matter) into [Cells](@genetics_resequenced:cell) of the same entity type.

A Pig's Organic Matter will produce Pig Cells, a Cow's Organic Matter will produce Cow Cells, and so on.
