The **Coal Generator** burns [furnace fuel](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Smelting#Fuel) and produces FE.

For every tick that the Generator is burning, it will produce 6 FE. A single [Coal](@coal) will burn for 1,600 ticks, producing a total of 9,600 FE.

The FE/t can be changed in the server config.
