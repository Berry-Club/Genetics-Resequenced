The **Mining Fatigue** Gene gives entities the Mining Fatigue potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Haste](@genetics_resequenced:haste) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
