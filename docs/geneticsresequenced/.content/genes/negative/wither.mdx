The **Wither** Gene gives entities the Wither potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Wither Proof](@genetics_resequenced:wither_proof) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
