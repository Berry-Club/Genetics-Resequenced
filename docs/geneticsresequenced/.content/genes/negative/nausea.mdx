The **Nausea** Gene gives entities the Nausea potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Milky](@genetics_resequenced:milky), [Meaty](@genetics_resequenced:meaty), or [Lay Eggs](@genetics_resequenced:lay_eggs) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
