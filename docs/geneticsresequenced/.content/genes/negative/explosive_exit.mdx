The **Explosive Exit** Gene causes entities to explode upon death, like a Creeper. The explosion does not effect blocks.

Players with the Gene will only explode if they're holding at least 5 Gunpowder in their inventory, which is consumed upon death.

## Obtaining

By default, you can get it from these sources:
- Creeper: 3/5
