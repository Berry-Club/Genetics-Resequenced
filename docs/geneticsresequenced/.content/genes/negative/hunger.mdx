The **Hunger** Gene gives entities the Hunger potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [No Hunger](@genetics_resequenced:no_hunger) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
