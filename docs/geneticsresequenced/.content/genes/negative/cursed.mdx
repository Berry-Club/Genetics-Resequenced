The **Cursed** Gene gives entities the Cursed potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Luck](@genetics_resequenced:luck) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
