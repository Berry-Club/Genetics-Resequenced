The **Weakness** Gene gives entities the Weakness potion effect.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Strength](@genetics_resequenced:strength) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
