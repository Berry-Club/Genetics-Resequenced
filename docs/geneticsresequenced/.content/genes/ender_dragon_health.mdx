The **Ender Dragon Health** Gene allows entities to offload incoming damage onto a held [Dragon Health Crystal](@geneticsresequenced:dragon_health_crystal).

## Obtaining

By default, You can get it from these sources:
- Allay: 5/8
- Endermite: 2/6
