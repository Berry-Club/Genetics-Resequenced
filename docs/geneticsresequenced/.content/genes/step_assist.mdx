The **Step Assist** Gene increases the entity's step height by 1 block.

## Obtaining

By Default, you can get it from these sources:
- Donkey: 3/18
- Horse: 3/16
- Llama: 3/8
- Polar Bear: 4/16
- Skeleton Horse: 4/12
- Zombie Horse: 4/12
