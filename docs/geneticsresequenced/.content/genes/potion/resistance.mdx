The **Resistance** Gene gives entities the Resistance potion effect.

## Obtaining

By default, you can get it from these sources:
- Drowned: 4/9
- Iron Golem: 4/19
- Ravager: 4/17
- Shulker: 4/11
- Zombie: 1/8
