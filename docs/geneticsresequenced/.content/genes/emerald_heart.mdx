The **Emerald Heart** Gene causes mobs to drop an Emerald upon death.

Additionally, players might have a chance of making certain sounds when chatting.

## Obtaining

By Default, you can get it from these sources:
- Evoker: 3/7
- Pillager: 2/7
- Villager: 2/7
- Vindicator: 2/10
- Zombie Villager: 4/9
