The **Wooly** Gene allows entities to be sheared to for Wool. Players can sneak right-click Shears to shear themselves, if they have the Gene.

Players that get sheared will lose the outer layers of their skin.

## Obtaining

By default, You can get it from these sources:
- Allay: 5/8
- Endermite: 2/6
