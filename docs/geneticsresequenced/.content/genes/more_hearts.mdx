The **More Hearts** Gene increases the entity's maximum health by 20 points (10 hearts).

It can be mutated into **More Hearts II** to further increase the maximum health by another 20 points (10 hearts).

## Obtaining

By default, you can get it from these sources:
- Enderman: 1/9
- Iron Golem: 2/19
- Ravager: 3/17
- Warden: 5/10
