The **Slimy Death** Gene makes it so that if the player dies, that death is canceled and it spawns some Support Slimes nearby.

Support Slimes have their owners heads, and will attack anything that's targeting their owner. They will despawn quickly if there's nothing to attack.

## Obtaining

By default, you can get it from these sources:
- Slime: 2/16
- Support Slime: 1/1
