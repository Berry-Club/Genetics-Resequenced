The **Wall Climbing** Gene allows the player to climb walls by walking into them.

Sneaking will stop you from climbing upwards, but will allow you to cling to ceilings.

## Obtaining

By default, you can get it from these sources:
- Spider: 2/13
- Cave Spider: 2/15
