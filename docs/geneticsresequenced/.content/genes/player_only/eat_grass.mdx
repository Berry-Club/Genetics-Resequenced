The **Eat Grass** Gene allows the player to right-click Grass Blocks with an empty hand to consume them, restoring 1 health and 1 saturation.

Grass Blocks and Mycelium will be turned into Dirt. Nylium of both varieties will be turned into Netherrack.

Eating grass also has some unique interactions with the [Wooly](../wooly) and [Cringe](../negative/cringe) Genes...

## Obtaining

By default, you can get it from these sources:
- Goat: 3/15
- Mooshroom: 4/11
- Sheep: 3/12
- Cow: 3/12
