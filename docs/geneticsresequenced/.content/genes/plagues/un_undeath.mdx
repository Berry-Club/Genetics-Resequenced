The **Un-Undeath** Gene kills the undead.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Scare Skeletons](@genetics_resequenced:scare_skeletons) or [Scare Zombies](@genetics_resequenced:scare_zombies) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
