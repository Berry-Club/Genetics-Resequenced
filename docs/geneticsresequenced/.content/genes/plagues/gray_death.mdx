The **Gray Death** Gene kills ageable mobs.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Resistance](@genetics_resequenced:resistance) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
