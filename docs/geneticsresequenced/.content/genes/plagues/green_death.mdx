The **Green Death** Gene kills Creepers.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Scare Creepers](@genetics_resequenced:scare_creepers) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
