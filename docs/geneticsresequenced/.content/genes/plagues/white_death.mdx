The **Green Death** Gene kills Monsters.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](../../items/potion/viral_agents) with a [Dragon's Breath](../../../../../../../white_death) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](../../blocks/machines/incubator).
