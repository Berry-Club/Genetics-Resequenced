The **White Death** Gene kills Monsters.

## Obtaining

Get the Gene by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with a [Dragon's Breath](@genetics_resequenced:dragons_breath) DNA Helix in an [Incubator](@genetics_resequenced:incubator).
