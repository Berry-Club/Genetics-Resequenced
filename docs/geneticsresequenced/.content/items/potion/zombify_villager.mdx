A **Potion of Zombify Villager** can be crafted by combining a [Potion of Viral Agents](@genetics_resequenced:viral_agents) with an [Emerald Heart](@genetics_resequenced:emerald_heart) Helix.

When used on a Villager, it will turn them into a Zombie Villager.
