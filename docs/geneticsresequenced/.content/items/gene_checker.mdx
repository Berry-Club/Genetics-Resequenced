The **Gene Checker** allows you to see what Genes a target has, as well as what Genes they can provide along with their weights.

For example, using it on a Pig will show something along the lines of:

> Pig has no Genes!
> Pig can provide the following Genes:
> - Basic with weight 5
> - Meaty with weight 2

Using it while not looking at anything will target yourself instead.
