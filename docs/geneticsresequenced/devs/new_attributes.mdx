# New Attributes

The mod uses a couple new Attributes to do various things. They're modified by Genes, but you can also modify them in other ways.

## Efficiency

Players have `geneticsresequenced:efficiency`, which increases the speed they mine at. It basically mimics the enchantment without actually requiring it.

It's used by the [Efficiency](@geneticsresequenced:efficiency) Gene.

## Base Looting

Players have `geneticsresequenced:base_looting`, which increases the loot they get from mobs. Basically, if they have the attribute at value 2, anything they kill acts like they had Looting 2 levels higher than they actually did.

It's used by the [Looting](@geneticsresequenced:looting) Gene.

## XP Drop Multiplier

Players have `geneticsresequenced:xp_drop_multiplier`, which multiplies the amount of XP mobs drop when killed by the player. For example, if the player has a value of 1.5, mobs will drop 50% more XP.

It's used by the [XP Boost](@geneticsresequenced:xp_boost) Gene.
